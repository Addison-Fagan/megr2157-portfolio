## My name is Addison Fagan, and I’m a mechanical engineering student at UNC Charlotte.
For as long as I can remember, I’ve been fascinated by how things work and obsessed with taking things apart just to figure out how to put them back together or build something entirely new. Joining a LEGO robotics club back in elementary school was the exact turning point that sparked it all for me, I remember looking at my first working mechanism and realizing right then that engineering was what I wanted to dedicate my life to. My ultimate goal is to design something that truly makes an impact in people's daily lives or even build a business of my own. I want to use the hard work I put into this challenging degree to make my parents proud and set a strong example for those who come after me.

To me, defending an engineering decision isn't simply about showing your math or clicking through a simulation; it’s about taking genuine personal ownership of your work. Every single design choice carries real-world weight, and overlooking a detail can lead to serious consequences. A failure might mean a company losing thousands of dollars, a damaged professional reputation, or, in the worst cases, catastrophic accidents where innocent people get hurt. Truly standing behind a decision means proactively thinking through every possible failure mode, testing your logic against real-world constraints, and having the integrity to prioritize safety and reliability above quick shortcuts.

Right now, I’ll be the first to admit that I don’t fully know how to defend complex engineering decisions at that high professional standard. That’s why I’m genuinely excited about taking this course. I want to build a solid technical foundation, learn how to run thorough risk assessments, and gain the confidence to back up my creative ideas with clear calculations and evidence. By the end of this semester, my goal is to develop the skills, discipline, and mindset necessary to become an engineer that teammates and clients can trust without hesitation.
